Why use the site?

Move provider calls out of separate workbooks.

Calls, facilities, follow-ups, and reports stay in one database. The site checks the business rules whenever a record is saved.

In the workbooksSeparate admin and user files

Copies could get out of sync.

→In the siteOne shared database

Everyone with access sees the same records.

In the workbooksThousands of formulas

Large recalculations slowed down normal work.

→In the siteCalculations run when needed

The result is checked when a call is saved.

In the workbooksLong sheets

Large lists were hard to search and review.

→In the siteSearchable pages

Lists are filtered and split into manageable pages.

In the workbooksRules tied to column positions

Moving a column could change a result.

→In the siteNamed fields with validation

The same checks apply no matter where a field appears on screen.

In the workbooksFollow-ups tracked by hand

Due work was easy to miss.

→In the siteA shared review queue

Follow-ups, duplicate calls, and data issues appear in one list.

In the workbooksReports saved as separate sheets

It was hard to see how an older total was calculated.

→In the siteReports and change history

Reports show their date range, and saved snapshots can be reviewed later.

That is the basic idea

Search providers, record calls, and follow up from the same place.

The demo uses made-up providers and authorizations so the full process can be tried safely.
